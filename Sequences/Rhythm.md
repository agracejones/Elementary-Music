## Steady Beat

#### Mastery looks like...
Students can keep a beat both internally and externally, progressing to instruments and sight singing as a group.
#### Grade-Level Approaches
K: Feeling the beat with songs and games
1: Keeping beat during songs with some guidance
2: Keeping beat during songs with little guidance
3: Associating rhythms with beat placement
4: Generating steady beat as a group
5: Generating steady beat individually
