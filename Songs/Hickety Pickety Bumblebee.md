## Lyrics
Hickety pickety bumblebee,  
Can you sing your name for me?  
(Student) My name is “—-”  
(Class echo) Your name is “—-”

## Variations/Modifications

## Other Notes
Bring in the crocheted bee and fly it around to different students to show whose turn it is. To avoid doubles or skips, have students stand in a circle and then sit down once they've said their name.
