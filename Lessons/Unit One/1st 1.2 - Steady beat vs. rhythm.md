| Beat Motion          | A beat is like the "heartbeat" of music. Draw four hearts on the board and tap them in a beat. Ask if the beat changes or stays the same. Beat always stays the same! Sing [[Hey, Hey, Look at Me!]] and demonstrate how the clapping, snapping, etc. keeps a beat. We can keep a beat in many ways, like clapping, patting, and stomping. In fact, many things in the world keep beats. Play the What Keeps a Beat video and ask students to identify the different objects. <br><br>Play the Radetzky March video and direct students to clap along. <br><br>Now introduce rhythm, which can still be clapped, patted, stomped, etc. but doesn't stay the same. Clap a few rhythms and have students copy. Bring up Beat or Rhythm game and have students repeat the audio with clapping, then stand on which side of the room correlates to their answer. | [Keeps a beat](https://musicplayonline.com/listening-kits/what-keeps-a-beat/?my_access_code=S886743&video_url=https%3A%2F%2Fvideos.sproutvideo.com%2Fembed%2Fee9bddb21a15edc464%2F84a810d853e4a0cd%3Fvolume%3D1)<br><br>[March](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bMV6xaRPvk&list=RD-bMV6xaRPvk&start_radio=1)<br><br>[Game](https://musicplayonline.com/games/beat-or-rhythm/?my_access_code=S886743) | 12 min     |
