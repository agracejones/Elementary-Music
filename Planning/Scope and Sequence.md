# Year Goals by Grade

## Kindergarten
- Fast and slow
- High and low
- Loud and soft
- Call and response
- Unison singing
- Instrument families
- Musical genres
- Steady beat and rhythm
- Melodic, rhythmic, and moving improvisation
- Kazoos
- Handbells

## First Grade
- Sol and mi
- Quarter note
- Quarter rest
- Eighth notes (ta-di)
- A and B sections
- Major and minor
- Improvisation
- Instrument families
- Kazoos
- Handbells
- Melodic contour

## Second Grade
- Sol, la, and mi
- Half note
- Duple and triple meter
- Improvisation
- Line and space notes
- AB and ABA form
- Pitched and non-pitched instruments
- Kazoos
- Handbells
- Listening activities

## Third Grade
- Do, re, mi, sol, la
- Whole notes, whole rests, and half rests
- Treble clef letter names
- Musical terminology (tempo, dynamics, etc.)
- Orchestra instruments
- Repeat symbol
- Canon
- Improvisation
- Ukuleles
- Boomwhackers

## Fourth Grade
- Do, re, mi, fa, sol, la, ti, do
- Time signatures
- Partner song
- Composing melodies
- Improvisation
- Ukuleles
- Boomwhackers
- Dotted half note and sixteenth notes
- World instruments

## Fifth Grade
- Two-part harmony
- Simple rhythmic dictation
- Improvisation
- Composition
- Instrument techniques
- Singing technique
- Glockenspiel
